สูตรที่ใช้

size = range × angle (rad) · small-angle / subtension
1 MOA = π / 10,800 rad (true MOA) → 1.047" @ 100 yd · 2.909 cm @ 100 m
1 MRAD (MIL) = 0.001 rad → 3.6" @ 100 yd · 10 cm @ 100 m
1 yd = 0.9144 m · 1 in = 2.54 cm · 1 MIL = 3.4377 MOA
